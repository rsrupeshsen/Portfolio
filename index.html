About

The short version.

I'm an engineer who works where AI, product and clients meet. I write code, test models, and help decide what an AI feature should do based on what the people using it need.

At Blue Machines AI I work on a voice AI platform that businesses use. I compare models for the team, write down what new features need to do, and trace client-reported bugs back to their cause so the engineers can fix them.

Before that, my internships were hands-on engineering: an accounting and inventory system in Django and PostgreSQL, machine learning models, and an AI assistant for farmers.

That mix means I can listen to a client's problem, work out what matters, and open the code myself. I'm based in Bengaluru.
